01

Autonomous Vehicles

Self-driving cars must make safe decisions in real time despite noisy sensors, model uncertainty and delayed or intermittent measurements.

  • Robust path-tracking and lateral/longitudinal control under parameter variations
  • Sampled-data and event-triggered control for in-vehicle networks
  • Sensor fusion and state estimation (LiDAR, camera, GNSS/IMU)
  • Motion planning with safety guarantees

02

Traffic Networks at Unsignalized Intersections

Connected and automated vehicles can cross intersections without traffic lights — if they coordinate. We study how to make this safe, efficient and scalable.

  • Cooperative intersection management via V2V/V2I communication
  • Conflict resolution, scheduling and optimal crossing order
  • Networked control under communication delays and packet loss
  • Mixed traffic with human-driven vehicles

03

Aerial Manipulators

Mounting a robotic arm on a drone turns it from an observer into an actor — but the arm's motion and contact forces strongly disturb flight.

  • Coupled dynamics modeling of multirotor + manipulator
  • Whole-body control and disturbance rejection during grasping
  • Contact-based inspection and aerial pick-and-place
  • Actuator saturation-aware controller design

04

Multi-Drone Cooperation

Teams of drones can cover larger areas, carry heavier loads and tolerate failures — provided they cooperate reliably over limited communication links.

  • Consensus and formation control of multi-agent systems
  • Sampled-data and event-triggered communication among agents
  • Cooperative transport and distributed task allocation
  • Collision avoidance and resilience to agent/link failures

05

Bio-Inspired Drones

Insects and birds fly with remarkable agility. Our flagship platform is the tailless flapping-wing micro aerial vehicle (FWMAV), which is highly nonlinear and naturally unstable.

  • Takagi–Sugeno fuzzy modeling of longitudinal and lateral FWMAV dynamics
  • LMI-based fuzzy controller design with comparison to PID baselines
  • Attitude stabilization without a tail via wing-kinematics modulation
  • Simulation (MATLAB/Simulink) to flight experiments
