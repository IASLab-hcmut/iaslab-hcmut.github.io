Principal Investigator

Dr. Nguyen Khanh Hieu

Research Professor, Chungnam National University

✉ Email 🎓 Google Scholar 📄 Publications

Dr. Hieu's research lies in signal processing and optimal control systems, with emphasis on sampled-data control algorithms for systems with uncertainties, random parameter variations and nonlinearities. His work spans Takagi–Sugeno fuzzy systems, Markovian jump systems, multi-agent systems and practical applications such as inverted pendulums, magnetic levitation, PMSG-based wind energy systems, DAB DC-DC converters and flapping-wing micro aerial vehicles.
